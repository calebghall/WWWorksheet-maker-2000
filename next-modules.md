# Proposed next modules

Ranked by standards gaps closed (45 gaps in standards-coverage.md) and reuse of existing drawing code.

1. Fractions (3.NF.A.1, 3.NF.A.2, 4.NF, 5.NF): shaded bar, circle and set models; fractions on a number line; equivalent and comparing; unlike-denominator add and subtract. Largest gap cluster; reuses the `vis` SVG kit and the `--c0` to `--c3` greyscale palette.
2. Geometry and Angles (3.G.A.1 to 3.G.A.3, 4.MD.C.5 to 4.MD.C.7, 4.G.A.1 to 4.G.A.3): classify polygons, partition into equal areas, identify and measure angles with a protractor, lines of symmetry, parallel and perpendicular lines.
3. Measurement with tools (3.MD.A.2, 3.MD.B.4, 4.MD.A.2, 5.MD.A.1): ruler to the half and quarter inch, scales and beakers for grams and liters, unit conversion, line plots with fractional data.
4. Area, Perimeter and Volume (3.MD.C.5 to 3.MD.D.8, 5.MD.C.3 to 5.MD.C.5): unit-square area, rectangle area and perimeter, unit-cube volume, V = l x w x h. Builds on `area.js`.
5. Money and Time word problems: coin pictures in "Count the money" (currently text only), make-change and dollar-and-cents totals using `vcoin()`, elapsed time on an analog clock.
6. Expressions and Patterns (5.OA.A.1 to 5.OA.B.3, 3.OA.A.1, 3.OA.A.2, 3.OA.D.9): parentheses and order of operations, writing expressions, two-rule patterns, multiplication chart patterns, interpreting products and quotients.
7. Decimals and Place Value (5.NBT): powers of 10, decimal read/write/expanded form, rounding decimals, decimal operations to hundredths, long division with two-digit divisors.
8. Coordinate Plane (5.G.A.1, 5.G.A.2): graph and read first-quadrant ordered pairs, real-world point plots.
9. Scaled Graphs (3.MD.B.3): scaled pictographs and bar graphs, draw-your-own graph with a grid.
